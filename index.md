## About Me

I am Xiyu Bao, a Postdoctoral at the School of Artifical Intelligence, Shandong University (SDU). I currently serve as a committee member of the CCF Virtual Reality and Visualization Technology Committee (TCVRV).

## Research Interests

- **Computation geometry:** Voronoi, splinegon decomposition
